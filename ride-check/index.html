Ride Check

Daily ride safety checks for amusement parks and ride operators, on iPhone and Android. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

What it does

  • Daily checks for every ride, from the operating manual or your own checklist, with pass or fail, fault notes, photos and a signed sign-off.
  • DOC tracking: each ride's Declaration of Operational Compliance and expiry date, with reminders before it runs out. Rides with an expired DOC can't be signed off.
  • Accident book: record accidents and first aid, with signatures and a locked record once signed off.
  • Food hygiene diary and staff training records.
  • Take payments at the ride: record cash sales, or take contactless card payments on your phone through Stripe.
  • Works offline. Your records are saved on your phone, with optional cloud backup.
Ride Check helps you keep records. It doesn't replace the manufacturer's instructions, your inspection body, or the checks and judgement of a competent person.
